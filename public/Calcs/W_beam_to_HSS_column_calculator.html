2. HSS column rectangular HSS, AISC v16.0

Pu and Mu are the column forces on the side of the joint with the higher compression stress (Eq. K1-6); they set Qf for face plastification and sidewall crippling / buckling. Leave at zero when the column is lightly loaded. Enter lend only when the beam frames near an open column end (§K1.4 Eq. K1-7).

4. Flange-to-HSS weld §J2 · §K5

both faces only
CJP develops the flange; the weld rows then report N/A and flange local yielding governs the weld line. Fillet welds use the effective length Be per face (Eq. K5-4); the portion in the HSS corner is a flare-bevel groove weld (Table J2.2, R = 2t). kds is not permitted for a single-sided fillet (DG24 2nd ed Ch. 3).

Basis and limits
  • Basis: beam flanges treated as a pair of transverse plates on the HSS face (AISC 360-22 Comm. K2; DG24 1st ed §4.4 / §7.1 and Ex 4.3; DG24 2nd ed Ch. 6). Puf = 12Mu/(d − tf).
  • Edition map: DG24 1st ed Table 7-2 Eqs. K1-2 / K1-3 / K1-4 / K1-5 / K1-6 are 360-10 numbers. In 360-22 they are §K1.2a Eq. K1-1 / K1-2 (effective widths), §J4 (yielding, shear), §J10.2 / J10.3 / J10.5 (sidewalls), Qf §K1.3 Eq. K1-4 / K1-6, end distance §K1.4 Eq. K1-7. There is no Eq. K1-7 yielding check and no Table K1.3A.
  • Applicability bands: punching 0.85B ≤ bfc ≤ B − 2t; sidewalls β ≥ 0.85 (Comm. K2); plastification β ≤ 0.85. Rows outside their band are shown as INFO and excluded from the governing D/C.
  • Flange wider than the flat or than B: see the classification row. Credited width is capped at B; corner welds are flare-bevel groove welds (Table J2.2, R = 2t; STI “Welding in HSS Corners”). DG24 2nd ed Ex 6.3 is cited per the 2026-09-23 handoff and has not been verified against a copy.
  • This connection is PR. FR stiffness, the web shear connection, fatigue (Appendix 3) and seismic (AISC 341 / 358) qualification are outside this calculator.
