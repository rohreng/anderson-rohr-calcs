Dowelled Embed Plate — Tension Dowels in Grouted CMU

Weldable deformed-bar dowels welded to a bearing plate — dowel tension, development in grout (Eq. 6-2) and the bar-to-plate weld

TMS 402-22 §6.1.6.3.1 §8.3.3.1 §9.1.4 §6.1.7.3.1 AISC 360-22 §J2.4 MDG 2022 REK-09

0. Code basis

Changes only the dowel tension check (ASD Fs, §8.3.3.1; SD φ = 0.90, Table 9.1.4) and the weld φ / Ω. Development length ld (Eq. 6-2) is the same in both.

1. Demand

From the connection analysis: the ASD combination (T) or the factored tension (Tu). Zero is allowed for a development-only check.

2. Wall & masonry grouted CMU

Cover is measured from the bar surface to the nearest masonry face. It enters K (§6.1.6.3.1) with the clear spacing between dowels and 9db.

3. Dowels weldable deformed bars

Spacing is used only when n ≥ 2 (clear spacing = s − db). Le is the bar length in grout below the plate; it must reach the full ld.

4. Plate layout & base metal

Dowel group centered on the plate: ep = (Lp − (n − 1)s)/2 (MDG REK-09: 3½ + 3 + 3½ = 10 in). Fy and Fu feed plate shear yielding / rupture at the weld (§J4.2). Plate flexure is not checked.

5. Bar-to-plate weld AISC 360-22 §J2

Table J2.2 note [a]: R < 3/8 in, only the reinforcing fillet is credited.
Fillet: Lwt = πdb, te = 0.707w, kds = 1.0 (Eq. J2-4). Flare: Lwt = 2Lw, te from Table J2.2 with R = db/2; for #3–#5 (R < 3/8 in) only the reinforcing fillet w is credited. Demand per bar = T/n.

Basis and limits
  • Basis: MDG 2022 Example REK-09 §5 (Dowelled Embed Plate Alternate, ASD and SD). Dowel tension per TMS 402-22 §8.3.3.1 (ASD, Tall = n·As·Fs) or Table 9.1.4 (SD, φTn = 0.90·n·As·fy).
  • Development: ld per §6.1.6.3.1 Eq. 6-2, not less than 12 in. The dowels are developed in full: TMS 402-22 has no reduction of ld for excess reinforcement. K uses the clear spacing between the dowels, as MDG REK-09 does (conservative).
  • Epoxy-coated bars: the Eq. 6-2 result is multiplied by 1.5; the factor does not apply to the 12 in minimum (§6.1.6.3.1 and commentary).
  • Load path (MDG REK-09 §5, REK-10): provide vertical reinforcement in the adjacent cell(s), lapped to the dowels, to carry T to the foundation. Size the lap with the masonry lap length calculator.
  • Weldability: bars ASTM A706, or a chemical analysis and carbon-equivalent submittal for the bars furnished (TMS 402-22 §6.1.7.3.1; TMS 602-22 Art. 3.4 B.7). Welding per AWS D1.4/D1.4M; procedure, qualification, preheat and inspection are the fabricator’s.
  • The weld is sized by AISC 360-22 §J2 strength only, Fnw = 0.60FEXX (Table J2.5), with kds = 1.0 on the all-around fillet (conservative). Weld metal governs over the A706 bar metal (Fu = 80 ksi), so the bar side of the weld is not checked separately.
  • Not checked here: beam bearing on the plate and masonry bearing (embed plate — beam bearing, masonry bearing / uplift); anchor shear; plate flexure; headed studs or anchor bolts (masonry anchor bolt).
