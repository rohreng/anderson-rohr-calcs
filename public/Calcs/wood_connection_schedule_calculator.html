Code basis: NDS 2018 (AWC, with Commentary), ASD. Demands V (lateral) and T (withdrawal) are entered at ASD level, per fastener or for the whole connection (toggle per row; qty = n × rows converts between them); the engine applies no load factors. CD per connection from its load case, capped at 1.6 (§11.3.2, Table 2.3.2). Reference values are computed from the yield-limit equations of Table 12.3.1A with the actual member thicknesses and penetrations (§12.3.1); the tabulated Tables 12A–12P are fixtures, not the method.
How to use: Add a connection to the table of its fastener type. The summary line carries the description, the fastener, the quantity (n × rows), the connection totals Vtotal / Ttotal (ASD), the total capacities Ztotal / Wtotal, D/C and status. The indented sub-row holds that connection's inputs in the Fastener Designer order — fastener, load (per fastener or total, toggle), Side Member A, Main Member B, pattern, factors; only the inputs that apply to its type and diameter are shown; factors that do not apply print as n/a with the reason. Details (▸) expands every intermediate in the FD report order: fastener properties, adjustment factors, penetration, fastener pattern, minimum spacing requirements, withdrawal, lateral (all six yield modes with Rd), combined, notes. Printing expands every connection. Main Member B = the member holding the point; Side Member A = the member under the head. t is the member dimension along the fastener (the depth it travels into that member); w is the face dimension across it. For a double-shear bolt the side block describes both (equal) side members.
Engineering notes — method, assumptions and limits
  • Method. Lateral reference values Z from the six yield modes of NDS 2018 Table 12.3.1A (four for double shear) with reduction terms Rd per Table 12.3.1B, dowel bearing strengths per Table 12.3.3 (rounded to 50 psi), Fyb per Table I1 by nominal diameter (override per row). Lag screws and wood screws use the root diameter Dr inside the yield equations only (§12.3.7.1); every other test uses the nominal D.
  • Penetration. The fastener length L is entered; penetrations are derived by intersecting the fastener with the members along its axis (side [0, ts], main [ts, ts + tm]). lm = p − E/2 for the tapered tip in the main member (§12.3.5.3); thread length in the main member for withdrawal by type (lag §12.2.1.2 excludes the tip, wood screw §12.2.2.2 keeps it, nail full length). Minimum penetration lag 4D excluding the tip (§12.1.4.6), wood screw and nail 6D (§12.1.5.6, §12.1.6.4); below the minimum the row fails and no capacity is reported. No p/8D or p/10D table-footnote scaling is applied.
  • Cg and CΔ (nominal D ≥ 1/4 in only) are computed from the layout as entered: rows parallel to the load, equal fasteners per row. Staggered rows are modelled per §11.3.6.2 (Fig. 11B): adjacent rows merge into one row for Cg when the row spacing g is less than 1/4 of the closest longitudinal offset between fasteners in adjacent rows (min(offset, s − offset)); the merged row carries 2n fasteners at s/2, and the lesser of the merged and separate-row Cg governs; every physical geometry check keeps the physical s and g. Load angle 0–90° per member: Feθ by Hankinson (Eq. 12.3-11), Kθ on the largest angle, end distance interpolated between the ∥ and ⊥ values (Commentary C12.5.1.2), edge distance and row spacing by the governing of the ∥/⊥ rules. Gross member areas per §11.3.6.3; a member loaded at an angle to grain uses the group width (rows − 1)·g measured along its grain, with the 3D minimum parallel-to-grain spacing for a single row; at an intermediate angle the lesser of the ∥ and ⊥ area interpretations governs. Hard geometry minimums (Tables 12.5.1A–E) fail the row; otherwise CΔ = min(1, end / full end, s / 4D). Across-grain spread over 5 in (((n − 1)·s + closest stagger offset)·sinθ + (rows − 1)·g·cosθ per member) fails the row unless shrinkage detailing is confirmed (§12.5.1.3).
  • Spacing for D < 1/4 in (nails, wood screws) is "sufficient to prevent splitting" per §12.1.5.7 / §12.1.6.5; the Commentary tables C12.1.5.7 / C12.1.6.6 (with or without prebored lead holes, wood or steel side) are used as the recommendation and any shortfall is an advisory warning, never a row failure. The Minimum Spacing Requirements block prints required / actual / ✓ or ⚠ per line for every row; for D ≥ 1/4 in the required values are the CΔ = 1.0 values of Tables 12.5.1A–D with the hard minima as the checks.
  • Withdrawal per Eq. 12.2-1/-2/-3 at the main-member G over the thread length in the main member; head pull-through per §12.2.5 Eq. 12.2-6 for round-head nails and wood screws through a wood side member, Wcap = min(W'·pt, WH'). Lag (hex head) and steel-side pull-through / pull-over are the engineer's check and count as unresolved. Nails and wood screws in end grain carry no withdrawal (§12.2.2.3, §12.2.3.3); lags in end grain use Ceg 0.75. Bolts have no NDS withdrawal value.
  • Combined loading §12.4: Eq. 12.4-1 (cos²/sin²) for lag and wood screws, Eq. 12.4-2 (cos/sin) for nails; D/C = max(V/Z', T/Wcap, √(V²+T²)/Z'α).
  • Toe-nails (§12.1.6.3, 30°, L/3 from the end): ls = min(ts, L/3), lm = min(L·cos30° − L/3, tm) (Commentary C12.5.4), withdrawal on the actual nail length in the main member; Ctn 0.83 lateral / 0.67 withdrawal; CM = 1.0 for toe-nail withdrawal (§12.5.4.1).
  • Not covered (v1): LRFD; SDS and other proprietary screws (ESR values); double-shear lags and wood screws; steel plate bearing, net section and pull-over (noted for the engineer, AISC); fastener steel tension in withdrawal (root area printed, §11.2.3); member net section, row tear-out and eccentricity (§11.2.3, Appendix E); split rings, shear plates, timber rivets, drift pins; multiple shear planes and asymmetric three-member joints (§12.3.8, §12.3.9); glulam limits Table 12.5.1F; a lag into a steel main member; concrete or masonry anchorage.
