Code basis: NDS 2018 (AWC, with Commentary), ASD. Demands V (lateral) and T (withdrawal) are entered per fastener at ASD level; the engine applies no load factors. CD per connection from its load case, capped at 1.6 (§11.3.2, Table 2.3.2). Reference values are computed from the yield-limit equations of Table 12.3.1A with the actual member thicknesses and penetrations (§12.3.1); the tabulated Tables 12A–12P are fixtures, not the method.
How to use: Add a connection to the table of its fastener type. The summary line carries the description, the required V and T per fastener (ASD), and the results. The indented sub-row holds that connection's inputs — only the inputs that apply to its type and diameter are shown; factors that do not apply print as n/a with the reason. Details (▸) expands every intermediate: penetrations, member properties, all six yield modes, Rd, the factor chain with clauses, withdrawal and pull-through, the combined check and the engineer notes. Printing expands every connection. Main member = the member holding the point; side member = the member under the head. For a double-shear bolt the side block describes both (equal) side members.
Engineering notes — method, assumptions and limits
  • Method. Lateral reference values Z from the six yield modes of NDS 2018 Table 12.3.1A (four for double shear) with reduction terms Rd per Table 12.3.1B, dowel bearing strengths per Table 12.3.3 (rounded to 50 psi), Fyb per Table I1 by nominal diameter (override per row). Lag screws and wood screws use the root diameter Dr inside the yield equations only (§12.3.7.1); every other test uses the nominal D.
  • Penetration. The fastener length L is entered; penetrations are derived by intersecting the fastener with the members along its axis (side [0, ts], main [ts, ts + tm]). lm = p − E/2 for the tapered tip in the main member (§12.3.5.3); thread length in the main member for withdrawal by type (lag §12.2.1.2 excludes the tip, wood screw §12.2.2.2 keeps it, nail full length). Minimum penetration lag 4D excluding the tip (§12.1.4.6), wood screw and nail 6D (§12.1.5.6, §12.1.6.4); below the minimum the row fails and no capacity is reported. No p/8D or p/10D table-footnote scaling is applied.
  • Cg and CΔ (nominal D ≥ 1/4 in only) are computed from the layout as entered: rows parallel to the load, equal fasteners per row, no stagger (§11.3.6.2 offsets are not modelled). Gross member areas per §11.3.6.3; a member loaded perpendicular to grain uses (rows − 1)·g, or 4D for one row. Hard geometry minimums (Tables 12.5.1A–E) fail the row; otherwise CΔ = min(1, end / full end, s / 4D). Across-grain spread over 5 in fails the row unless shrinkage detailing is confirmed (§12.5.1.3).
  • Withdrawal per Eq. 12.2-1/-2/-3 at the main-member G over the thread length in the main member; head pull-through per §12.2.5 Eq. 12.2-6 for round-head nails and wood screws through a wood side member, Wcap = min(W'·pt, WH'). Lag (hex head) and steel-side pull-through / pull-over are the engineer's check and count as unresolved. Nails and wood screws in end grain carry no withdrawal (§12.2.2.3, §12.2.3.3); lags in end grain use Ceg 0.75. Bolts have no NDS withdrawal value.
  • Combined loading §12.4: Eq. 12.4-1 (cos²/sin²) for lag and wood screws, Eq. 12.4-2 (cos/sin) for nails; D/C = max(V/Z', T/Wcap, √(V²+T²)/Z'α).
  • Toe-nails (§12.1.6.3, 30°, L/3 from the end): ls = min(ts, L/3), lm = min(L·cos30° − L/3, tm) (Commentary C12.5.4), withdrawal on the actual nail length in the main member; Ctn 0.83 lateral / 0.67 withdrawal; CM = 1.0 for toe-nail withdrawal (§12.5.4.1).
  • Not covered (v1): LRFD; SDS and other proprietary screws (ESR values); double-shear lags and wood screws; load-to-grain angles other than 0°/90°; staggered layouts; steel plate bearing, net section and pull-over (noted for the engineer, AISC); fastener steel tension in withdrawal (root area printed, §11.2.3); member net section, row tear-out and eccentricity (§11.2.3, Appendix E); split rings, shear plates, timber rivets, drift pins; multiple shear planes and asymmetric three-member joints (§12.3.8, §12.3.9); glulam limits Table 12.5.1F; a lag into a steel main member; concrete or masonry anchorage.
  • Sources: NDS 2018 with Commentary (AWC), page cites printed/PDF as listed in each row's cites; NDS Supplement Tables 4A/4B (E); Appendix I (Fyb), Appendix L (fastener dimensions).
