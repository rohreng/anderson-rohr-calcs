Composite action informational only — cannot pass

Composite action requires a positive zero-slip connection — a shear plate welded to the channel web. Reamed holes and an epoxy-filled masonry annulus are not sufficient: they leave the channel-web clearance, which is the interface the longitudinal shear actually crosses. No weld or shear-plate design exists in this calculator, and A307 / A449 / A193-B7 rods are not generically field-weldable. Composite output is therefore informational, carries no PASS status, and is excluded from the optimiser.
