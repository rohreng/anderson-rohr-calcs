Stepped Parapets (roof level, ASCE 7-16 §27.3.4)▶

pp = qp·GCpn (Eq. 27.3-3): GCpn = +1.5 on the windward face, 1.0 on the leeward face, qp at the top of the highest parapet (h + hp,max). The typical parapet (hp,typ along the full face) is already inside Vx/Vy from the MWFRS; each step adds only its height above typical: F = GCpn·qp·(h − hp,typ)·width. N/S-face steps load Wind-Y; E/W-face steps load Wind-X. Both wind senses are analyzed and enveloped.

Flat roof, common base only: every parapet height above is measured from the same roof datum h the MWFRS used. Steps on a sloped (gable / hip / monoslope) roof, or without the common-base box checked, are an error — no results. Sloped roofs stay on the MWFRS typical-parapet load.
