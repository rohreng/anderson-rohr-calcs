Engineering Notes:
  • Load Path: Wall zone loads (plf × length = kips) are distributed in proportion to span length — equivalent to a uniform intensity w = ΣP/ΣL applied to every span. The Wall DL and Wall LL columns are total kips carried by that span; the derived wwall column shows the resulting klf. Editing either cell marks it as an override and it is no longer auto-distributed. Refine with point-load analysis for critical cases.
  • Load Combinations: Grade beams take the greater of 1.4D and 1.2D + 1.6(L+S); the governing combination is reported per beam. Snow arrives merged into L from the stacked calculator and is factored at 1.6, which is conservative relative to ACI 5.3.1(c)–(d). Pier ASD capacity uses the straight sum D + (L+S), conservative relative to ASCE 7-22 §2.4.1. Gravity only — no wind, seismic, uplift or lateral load combinations are evaluated anywhere in this tool.
  • Grade Beam Continuity: Negative moment and top steel at the piers are always checked when there are two or more spans — against ACI 318-19 Table 6.5.2 where every §6.5.1 condition is satisfied, otherwise against a wuL²/8 envelope. Mid-span uses wuL²/8 unless the continuous box is ticked and all §6.5.1 conditions pass, in which case wuln²/11 (end) and /16 (interior) apply. Shear takes the greater of wuL/2 and 1.15wuln/2 (Table 6.5.4). Clear span ln is derived from the adjacent shaft diameters, so Span is taken as centre-to-centre. End-span positive moment uses the discontinuous end unrestrained coefficient (/11), since a drilled shaft provides negligible rotational restraint. Negative moment at a shared support uses each span's own ln rather than the §6.5.3 average of adjacent clear spans; under the 1.2× span-ratio gate the longer span's check envelopes the code value where top steel is uniform, but review this if you cut As,top on one span only.
  • Shear: Vc per ACI 318-19 Table 22.5.5.1 including the size-effect factor λs = √(2/(1+d/10)) of §22.5.5.1.3, capped at 5λ√f'cbwd. λs is taken as 1.0 only when minimum stirrups are declared. Av,min per §9.6.3.1 is triggered whenever Vu > φλ√f'cbwd, well below φVc. λ = 1.0 (normalweight).
  • Effective depth: d = h − cover − stirrup bar − db/2.
  • Pier Embedment: Minimum total depth = depth to bearing + minimum penetration + active penetration required. Round up to 0.5 ft increments in the field.
  • Belled Piers: End bearing on bell face area. Skin friction excludes the bell flare height (60° bell) plus one shaft diameter above it, and is credited only beyond minimum penetration.
  • Pier structural capacity: Psvc ≤ 0.33f'cAg on the shaft per IBC 2021 §1810.3.2.6. Slenderness is neglected — the shaft is assumed laterally braced by the surrounding soil for its full length. Minimum longitudinal reinforcement and ties per IBC §1810.3.9 are not evaluated. Pier self-weight is excluded from Psvc (conventionally offset by the weight of excavated soil).
  • Judgment items left to the engineer: full end bearing and full skin friction are taken simultaneously at the same displacement — many geotechnical reports prohibit this, so confirm against the report. Total wall length is not reconciled against total span length. Lateral load, uplift and downdrag are out of scope.
  • Stacked Calc Link: Use "Export to Foundation Design" in the Stacked Headers & Studs Calc to auto-populate Section A with lowest-floor accumulated wall loads.
