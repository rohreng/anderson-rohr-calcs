  Engineering Notes — NDS 2018 / ASCE 7-16 / IBC 2021, ASD:
  
    
    
  • Load combinations. Every member is checked against the ASCE 7-16 §2.4.1 envelope, each
      combination carrying its own NDS Table 2.3.2 CD: D 0.9 · D+L 1.0 · D+S / D+0.75L+0.75S 1.15 ·
      D+Lr / D+0.75L+0.75Lr 1.25 · D+0.6W, D+0.75L+0.75(0.6W)+0.75(Lr or S) and
      0.6D+0.6W 1.6. The governing combination is reported for each check. Lr and S are alternatives and are never
      summed. Rain (R) is not an input and is not enveloped. Headers and jamb packs carry no wind, so
      combinations 5–7 are reported n/a for them. No live-load reduction (§4.7 / §4.8) is taken.
    • 
    
  • Wind. Both wind inputs are strength-level ASCE 7-16 §30.3 component-and-cladding
      pressures — the value the C&C calculator hands over. The engine applies the 0.6 of combinations 5–7;
      do not pre-factor them.
    • 
    
  • Unbraced lengths. Strong axis: header = the span, jamb pack = the top-of-opening (trimmer) height,
      king and wall stud = the story height. Weak axis: the bracing spacing entered above, 48 in by default — blocking or
      sheathing, and it must be shown on the drawings as a construction note. Enter 0 only where the sheathing genuinely braces
      the member continuously (NDS A.11.3). Both axes are computed and the smaller CP governs; the true
      le/d is printed for each axis and anything above 50 is refused, not clamped (§3.7.1.4).
      RB is likewise refused above 50 (§3.3.3.7).
    • 
    
  • CL. Computed unbraced per §3.3.3 unless the row's "Br" box is ticked, which invokes
      §3.3.3.3 (compression edge supported throughout its length) and sets CL=1.0. le is the
      Table 3.3.3 single-span uniform row: 2.06 lu below lu/d = 7, 1.63 lu + 3d at or above.
    • 
    
  • Cr. 1.15 on wall studs at ≤ 24 in o.c. (§4.3.9 — 3+ members, ≤ 24 in o.c.,
      joined by load-distributing sheathing; the predicate is printed with the check). 1.0 on headers, jamb packs and
      king studs, which do not meet it.
    • 
    
  • King studs take half a stud spacing of the header row's accumulated tributary gravity — the
      wall strip plus the roof and floor tributaries entered on that row — and the wind over (opening/2 + spacing/2)
      for the full story height. Where more than one king is provided each is checked for the whole strip;
      the count is redundancy, not a division of the demand. The jamb pack carries 100 % of the header reaction. Kings bend about the
      strong axis with CL=1.0 (sheathed compression edge). Eq. 3.9-3 is refused, never zeroed,
      when fc reaches FcE1. Top of opening above the story height is an input error, not a silent rewrite.
    • 
    
  • Deflection (IBC 2021 Table 1604.3). Headers: L/360 on the live row (L with the larger of Lr
      or S) and L/240 on the total row, using 0.5D when "Dry at installation" is ticked (footnote d). Wall studs: H/240 brittle
      finish or H/120 flexible, at the wind deflection basis selected above (footnote f permits 0.42W).
    • 
    
  • Bearing. Fc⊥ takes no CD (Table 2.3.2 fn. 1), so the largest
      unfactored combination governs it. With # Jambs left at 0 the pack is designed for this interface as well as
      for axial, so the auto-selected count is never smaller than R / (Fc⊥ · beff · 1.5). Cb=1.0 everywhere here — §3.10.4 excludes bearings at
      the end of a member. Header bearing length = (# jambs) × 1.5 in; jamb packs and studs are also checked on the
      bottom plate using the plate species selected above.
    • 
    
  • Built-up headers. Equal load sharing between plies is assumed and no ply fastening is
      designed — specify the nailing or bolting separately. Header self-weight is not added automatically; type
      it into DLadd.
    • 
    
  • Stacking. Header stacks and stud rows are correlated across floors by a stable id, not by label text.
      A stack that skips a level is an input error unless that row is marked "Tr" (transfer). Changing species, wall size,
      story height, wind or any load clears every result panel so a printed sheet cannot show superseded numbers.
    • 
    
  • Scope. No.2 grade, 2x sawn lumber only; dry service and normal temperature; concrete, foundations and
      the connection of the header to its supports are out of scope. Shear is taken at the reaction (the §3.4.3.1(a)
      reduction is not used — conservative). Jamb packs are assumed concentrically loaded.
    • 
    
  • Export. "Export to Foundation Design" sends the lowest floor's accumulated D, L, Lr and S
      (plf) and the jamb point reactions (lb) to the integrated grade beam & pier calculator.
