Engineering Notes & Assumptions:
  • WRI beam depth formula d = ∛(664 M lc / B) — empirical WRI constant for reinforced concrete grade beams; units: M in kip-ft, lc in ft, B in in, d in in
  • The WRI moment M and shear V are service-level. A blanket load factor of 1.6 is applied to both (Mu,beam = 1.6 Mbeam, Vu = 1.6 wL’lc/N) — conservative relative to the ACI 318-14/19 §5.3.1 combinations because w is predominantly dead load
  • Flexure per ACI 318-14/19 §22.2 rectangular stress block. ϕ is computed from the net tensile strain per Table 21.2.2 (ϕ = 0.90 only when εt ≥ 0.005); a warning is shown when the section is not tension-controlled
  • Two effective depths are used: dbot = h − cover for bottom steel and shear, dtop = h − cover − 1″ for top steel (top bars sit one bar layer below the top face)
  • Same moment demand used for both top and bottom steel (WRI assumes both faces experience uplift/settlement reversals)
  • Minimum flexural steel per ACI 318-14/19 §9.6.1.2 is checked, with the §9.6.1.3 waiver: the governing area is max(As,req, min(As,min, 4/3 As,req)). A minimum of two bars top and two bottom is enforced in every beam per WRI TF700 p.16
  • Deflection uses the WRI TF700 Appendix A cantilever formula Δ = 1728 w L’ lc4 / (4 Ec,creep ΣI), with Ec,creep = Ec/2 for long-term creep (equivalent to λΔ ≈ 2.0, ACI 318-14/19 §24.2.4) and ΣI taken as all beams in the direction, rectangular, slab flange ignored
  • One-way shear is checked per ACI 318-14/19 §22.5.5.1 (ϕVc = 0.75 × 2√f’cbwd, λ = 1.0). Where Vu exceeds ϕVc but stays within the §22.5.1.2 limit, the required Av/s and a #3 U-stirrup spacing are reported; this is a reinforcement requirement, not a section failure, and does not fail the summary
  • Per WRI TF700 Note “A” (detailing minimum, independent of the calculated shear check above): grade beams 36–54″ deep — #3 stirrups @ 24″ o.c. max; 54–72″ deep — #3 stirrups @ 18″ o.c. max; over 72″ deep — engineer review required. In addition, provide one #3 horizontal bar each face per 18″ of depth beyond 36″
  • Slab reinforcement As,slab = Asfy/fy (Asfy entered by PI per WRI Fig 5), same each way; #3 bar spacing capped at 18″ o.c. per Fig 18; an equivalent-area WWR is an acceptable substitute per WRI TF705
  • Bar spacing and cover per ACI 318-14/19 §25.2 must still be confirmed for final detailing
